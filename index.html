Model assumptions
Gravity is fixed at g = 9.81 m/s² downward. Air resistance and the ball's size are ignored. The launch point is height h above the ground.
For angle prediction, two valid launch angles may exist. Both will be listed and drawn when possible. Maximum height and impact data are shown for each solution.

Equations used
x(t) = v₀ cos(θ) · t
y(t) = h + v₀ sin(θ) · t − ½gt²
g = 9.81 m/s²
ymax = h + (v₀ sin θ)² / (2g)   (when v₀ sin θ > 0)
vx = v₀ cos θ   (constant)
vy(t) = v₀ sin θ − g·t
vimpact = √(vx² + vy²)
φimpact = atan(|vy| / vx)
